NB: for each of the involved countries, the worst team in the group 
    pahes is to be replaced for 1999 with the national champions in
    1998: Independiente (Argentina), Corinthians (Brazil), Universidad
    de Chile (Chile), Cerro Porteño (Paraguay) and Peñarol (Uruguay)
    are the teams "relegated" by this rule.  Since in all cases the 
    1998 champions were already among the 20 'elect' clubs, no team
    was actually replaced (Corinthians in fact replace themselves as
    Brazilian champions 1998).
