Copa Sul-Minas 2000

Note: The teams from the state of Minas Gerais decided to withdraw from the Copa
Centro-Oeste to a more profitable tournament.  Although the state of Minas Gerais
does not have a border with any of the 3 southernmost states, CBF and Clube dos 
13 decided to realocate Atlético, Cruzeiro and América to Copa Sul.  Hence the 
tournament was renamed to Copa Sul-Minas.
